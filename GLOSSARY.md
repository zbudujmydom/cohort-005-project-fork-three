# Cadence

A course platform where instructors publish video courses and students buy them, work through their lessons, and can ask for their money back.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person who buys courses and works through their lessons.
_Avoid_: Learner

**Instructor**:
A person who publishes courses and owns the ones they publish.

**Admin**:
A member of the Cadence team who runs the platform, including deciding refund requests.

**Staff**:
For a given course, its own instructor or any admin: the people who speak for that course.

**Team owner**:
The person who buys a team licence and hands out its seats.
_Avoid_: Team admin

### Buying and refunding

**Purchase**:
A payment for a course: either a student buying it for themselves, or a team owner buying a team licence.

**Enrollment**:
A student's access to a course, coming from a purchase or a claimed seat. Ending an enrollment is what "losing access" means.
_Avoid_: Access (as a noun for the record)

**Regional price**:
A lower price for students buying from certain countries. A purchase at a regional price can only be used, and its seats only claimed, from that country.
_Avoid_: PPP, purchasing-power discount

**Refund request**:
The buyer's ask, with their reason, to get their money back for a purchase. It stays open until an admin approves or declines it; a declined request returns nothing.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, reversal request

**Refund**:
The full amount paid for a purchase, returned to the buyer when their refund request is approved. A refund ends the enrollment, or voids the seats, that the purchase paid for.
_Avoid_: Reversal, cancellation

**Refund window**:
The fourteen calendar days, counted from the day of purchase and including the last day, during which the buyer may make a refund request.

**Watch time**:
How far a student has got through a course's videos: the furthest point reached in each lesson, added up across the course. Skipping ahead counts as watched.

**Team licence**:
One purchase of several seats in a course, bought by a team owner for others to use. Refunding it voids all of its seats.

**Seat**:
One student's access to a course, taken from a team licence. A seat is claimed when a student redeems it, and the student who claims it never paid for it.
_Avoid_: Coupon
